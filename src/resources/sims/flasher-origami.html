Flasher origami

The crease pattern of the self-deploying solar array (Chen et al., 2019): a core of n sides and c layers of facets, wrapping round the core as it folds. Play opens and closes it.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. The core polygon stays put. The fold is kinematic: each strip of the pattern tilts up, pleats against its neighbours and winds round the core, until every strip stands on edge in a spiral stack of height equal to its width. The pattern is not rigidly foldable, so the facets next to the spines stretch a little part-way.

Pattern: Chen, Bilal, Lang, Daraio and Shea (2019), Appendix B.
