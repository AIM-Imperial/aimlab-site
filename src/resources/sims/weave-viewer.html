Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, F to fit. weft along x, warp along y, wup along z. Each yarn is t ei + Acos(2πt/λ+Φij) ej + Acos(2πt/λ+Φij') ej', with AcosΦ = a c at its vertex. A = a is the in-phase family, A = √2 a the helical one; in between the yarns are elliptical helices. Yellow markers are interlacings whose over/under is the opposite of the prescribed one; for the helical family the first ones appear at a = √2 λ/16 ≈ 0.088 λ. Lengths are in units of the wavelength λ; the vertex spacing is λ/2.
