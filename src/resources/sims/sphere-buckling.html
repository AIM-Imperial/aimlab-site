Sphere buckling

A thin spherical shell under uniform external pressure stays spherical below the classical critical pressure and buckles at and above it. The shape drawn is one axisymmetric mode, with ring-shaped waves centred on a pole; many other shapes share the same classical pressure. Note the amplitude is arbitrary. Real spherical shells buckle well below this pressure because of small imperfections.
