Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, F to fit. The red dot is the origin (0, 0, 0) when “Red origin dot” is on; the arrows beside it point along x, y and z. weft along x, warp along y. Each yarn is t ei ± acos(2πt/λ) ez: it passes every vertex a above or below the plane, the weft at a c and the warp at −a c, where c = ±1 alternates from vertex to vertex like a checkerboard. “Weft over” sets c = 1 at the origin, “Warp over” c = −1. Lengths are in units of the wavelength λ; the vertex spacing is λ/2. Lines from axis to centerline join each yarn's lattice line to its centerline every λ/32; in the plane weave they all run along z, in the yarn's own plane. Half a cell along a direction is a single line of vertices, cut λ/4 either side of it, so the yarns along that direction are λ/2 long.
