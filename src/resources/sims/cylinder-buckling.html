Cylinder buckling

A thin cylinder under axial compression stays straight below the classical critical load and buckles into ring-shaped (axisymmetric) waves at and above it. Note the amplitude is arbitrary. Many other buckling shapes share the same classical load, and real cylinders buckle at a fraction of it because of small imperfections.
