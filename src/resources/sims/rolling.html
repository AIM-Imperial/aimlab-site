Flat rolling

Two rolls turn in opposite directions and pull a strip through the gap between them. Close the gap to make the strip thinner. Drag the view to turn it.

Close the roll gap

Gap open: no changeGap closed: much thinner

Process

What to notice

  • The strip leaves thinner. Its width hardly changes, so it becomes longer.
  • The squares on the top become longer. The strip leaves faster than it enters, because the volume of metal does not change.
  • Friction between the rolls and the strip pulls the strip into the gap.
  • Cold rolling flattens and stretches the grains. In hot rolling, new small grains form after the gap (Kalpakjian and Schmid, Fig. 13.6).
