Torsion of a Circular Shaft

A circular shaft fixed at one end and twisted by a torque at the other. The surface lines are straight when unloaded — twist turns them into helices.

Series: Cantilever bending · Axial stretching · Torsion · Shear beam · Buckling · Plate stretching
