## Imperial scholarships

| Scheme | Deadline | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [President's PhD Scholarship](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/postgraduate-doctoral/grants-scholarships/presidents-phd/) | Three rounds, closing in **early November**, **mid-January**, and **early March** | Select it in the funding section of your PhD application. |

| [Imperial Marshall Scholarships](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/postgraduate-doctoral/grants-scholarships/international-scholarship-collaborations/ims/) | Set by the Marshall Commission, usually **September** | For US citizens with a first degree from a US institution and a grade point average of at least 3.7 who have not previously studied at a UK university. |

## National scholarships

Many countries fund their own citizens to study for a doctorate abroad, and for
international applicants this is often the most practical route. Examples include the
China Scholarship Council, Indonesia's LPDP, Turkey's YLSY programme, Brazil's CAPES,
and Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education. If you hold or intend to apply for one, name
the scheme in your first message.

## Supplementary awards

These supplement an existing award or cover specific costs.

- [Amelia Earhart Fellowship](https://www.zonta.org/Web/Web/Programs/Education/Amelia_Earhart_Fellowship.aspx) - for women pursuing doctoral degrees in aerospace engineering and space sciences
- Royal Aeronautical Society Centennial Scholarship Fund
- Institution of Mechanical Engineers postgraduate awards
- Armourers and Brasiers' Company awards in materials science
- Livery company and charitable trust awards, indexed on the College's
  [charities and trusts page](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/other-funding-sources/charities-trusts/)

## Timing

For a start in October, contact us in the summer of the previous year and apply in the
November or January round. Contact Lisa Kelly, PhD Administrator (Admissions), at
[l.kelly@imperial.ac.uk](mailto:l.kelly@imperial.ac.uk) with questions about the
application process.
