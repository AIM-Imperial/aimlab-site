The Architected Intelligent Matter (AIM) Laboratory explores intelligent matter
at the intersection of mechanics, materials, and geometry. We design materials and structures whose behaviour - how they deploy, deform, respond, and remember - is written into their architecture. Our [research vision](/research/vision/) describes the three pillars of this work; our [projects](/research/) and [publications](/publications/) document the results.

We are driven by curiosity, a passion for science, and a belief that doing research is rewarding and enjoyable. We treat one another with respect and honesty: we give direct feedback, we credit each other's contributions, and we have open, courageous conversations. We are committed to building a group that is diverse and inclusive, where people of every background, perspective, and experience feel they belong and can do their best work. We care about each other's wellbeing and growth, and we believe a supportive, welcoming environment is critical to doing excellent science.

The group was founded at the University of Houston in September 2021. Since June 2026 it has been based in the [Department of Aeronautics](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/aeronautics/) at Imperial
College London, on the South Kensington campus. The studio side of the lab explores the same ideas [through the practice of art](/gallery/).
